How to Record

  1. Click any video card above to open it
  2. Press F12 to open DevTools, then Ctrl+Shift+M for device mode
  3. Set dimensions to 1080 x 1080 (square for social media)
  4. Start your screen recorder (Windows: Win+G, or use OBS/Loom)
  5. Press F5 to refresh and restart the animation
  6. Record until the progress bar reaches 100%
